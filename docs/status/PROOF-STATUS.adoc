= Proof Status — Lintel
:revdate: 2026-11-09

This is Lintel's proof-status file in the sense of
`standards/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc` §8.5: the per-claim record of status
tokens, Proof Witness Grades (PWG) and recheck legs. The claims themselves, with
their statements and artefacts, are the table in link:../../README.adoc[README]
(L-1 to L-7, A-1). The machine copy is
link:../../verification/receipts/agda-pwg_praxis.deed[`verification/receipts/agda-pwg_praxis.deed`],
generated by `scripts/grade-receipt.sh`; CI fails if it drifts from the tree, the
import cone or the README's claim IDs.

Until 2026-11-09 this file was the RSR template's placeholder ("0% proven", Idris
ABI rows). That never described this repository. The README's claim table was
the only accurate record, and the estate census (mathesis) read this file.

== The gate

Agda 2.6.4.3 (Debian `agda-bin=2.6.4.3-1+b2`), builtins only plus the pinned
sibling, under the flags of `scripts/proof-flags.sh`:
`--safe --without-K --cubical-compatible --no-libraries --ignore-interfaces
--double-check -W error`. That is the canonical §2.1 invocation and four stronger
flags. `scripts/check.sh` checks, in order:

. coverage: every module under `src/Lintel` is imported by `Lintel.All`;
. the sibling at its pin, `hyperpolymath/secret-types` `2f342bc`, all of
  `SecretTypes.All` under the same flags;
. `Lintel.All`;
. the 5 positive controls under `tests/accept`;
. the 4 expected rejections under `tests/reject`, each refused at its `invalid`
  declaration with a type mismatch, never a scope or parse error;
. a negative control: the rejection check, pointed at each positive control,
  reports it accepted, so step 5 cannot pass vacuously.

Trust base: no postulates, no escape hatches, no library, no axioms (`--safe`, and
the counts in the receipt). A-1 is a hypothesis of the threat model, not an axiom
of any proof: no proof assumes it, and `Lintel.Instance.collusion-gap` proves
what happens without it.

`foundation_diverse` is false for every row: no witness outside Agda exists.

*Why not higher.* `PWG-2i` needs one of the §2 legs (Rocq or Lean) to count:
kernel-accepted and `RECONCILED` against the Agda statement. Neither exists.
`unavailable` is the opening state, stated: no Agda→Dedukti transport has been
attempted and no hand re-proof exists. `PWG-3` is not a discharge (§8.4): each
`PWG-3` row still owes both legs.

*The template skeletons are not the legs.* `verification/proofs/{agda,coq,lean4,idris2,tlaplus}`
are RSR template boilerplate. They prove nothing about Lintel, and no workflow
runs them.

*How the canonical invocation was met (2026-11-09).* Three lemmas in
`Lintel.Lattice` matched a second `_⊒_` proof whose indices were already fixed:
`⊒-trans`, `⊒-antisym` and `∨-least`. That match needs constructor injectivity,
which `--cubical-compatible` refuses. They now return that proof unmatched, or go
through `⊒-open`. In `Lintel.Composition`, `writes-flow` matched the typing
derivation and the `Writes` derivation together on one `Cmd` index. It now reads
`Writes` into `Writes′`, the same relation computed by recursion on the command,
and recurses on the typing derivation alone. `Writes`, the relation L-7 is stated
over, is unchanged, as is every statement. The sibling needed the matching change,
hyperpolymath/secret-types#26 (`just-injective`), which is why the pin is that
branch's commit until it merges.
