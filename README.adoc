A privacy lattice for oblivious DNS (formerly oDNS), proved in Agda: per-field reader-set labels under which self-defeating privacy compositions are refused, built to instantiate secret-types' noninterference theorem.

== What this is

A Lintel label names the oblivious-DNS roles (client, proxy, target) that may
observe a field, and combining fields intersects their readers. The client's
address may reach the proxy and the query name may reach the target, but the
two together are the client's alone. A composition that would show one
non-client role both of them therefore needs a flow the lattice refuses. The
proofs are in Agda (`--safe --without-K`). The noninterference theorem the
lattice is built to instantiate comes from
link:https://github.com/hyperpolymath/secret-types[hyperpolymath/secret-types],
consumed at a pinned commit.

== Privacy lattice: proof status

Lintel labels each field with the set of oblivious-DNS roles (client, proxy,
target) allowed to observe it. Combining fields intersects their readers, so the
client's address (client and proxy) combined with the query name (client and
target) is the client's alone. The guarantee is secret-types'
termination-insensitive noninterference (TINI) at this lattice, with one
observer per role: two finishing runs that agree on what a role may observe end
up agreeing on it. It holds for programs over the two fields, not only for
labels (L-6, L-7), under the non-collusion assumption A-1.

[cols="1,4,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Claim | Status | Artefact

| L-1
| A lattice, not just a poset. ⊑ is a decidable partial order, ⊔ and ⊓ are least upper and greatest lower bounds, `anyone` is least, and `no-one` is greatest and never flows down.
| PROVEN
| `src/Lintel/Lattice.agda`: `⊑-refl`, `⊑-trans`, `⊑-antisym`, `⊔-upper₁`/`₂`, `⊔-least`, `⊓-lower₁`/`₂`, `⊓-greatest`, `anyone-least`, `no-one-greatest`, `no-one-⋢-anyone`, `_⊑?_`

| L-2
| ⊑ is reverse inclusion of reader sets. A role sees a label exactly when the label may flow to that role's observer level.
| PROVEN
| `⊑-readers`, `readers-⊑`, `observes-flow`, `flow-observes`

| L-3
| The laws secret-types' generic TINI needs hold here: `⊔-upper₁`, `⊔-upper₂` and `⊑-trans` (secret-types N-6, the certified census). The two-point laws of N-6's earlier reading hold too.
| PROVEN
| `⊔-upper₁`/`₂`, `⊑-trans`, packed as `Lintel.Instance.reader-sets`; earlier reading: `⊔-anyone₁`/`₂` (⊔-L₁/₂), `no-one-⊔` (H ⊔ y = H), `anyone-⊔` (L ⊔ y = y)

| L-4
| Address and query name each reach their own role. Composed, only the client may observe them.
| PROVEN
| `address-to-proxy`, `qname-to-target`, `link`, `link-confined`

| L-5
| The typechecker refuses the self-defeating compositions and accepts the client's.
| TESTED
| `tests/reject/LinkToProxy.agda`, `tests/reject/LinkToTarget.agda`, `tests/accept/LinkToClient.agda`, run by `scripts/check.sh` with a negative control

| L-6
| `tini` holds at this lattice, with `lo` the address and `hi` the query name, for each role's observer and for any two roles observing together: secret-types' N-7 statement, agreement read at that observer.
| PROVEN
| `src/Lintel/Instance.agda`: `reader-sets` (an instance of secret-types' `Lattice` record, link:https://github.com/hyperpolymath/secret-types/issues/9[secret-types#9]), `field-label`, `tini-per-role`, `tini-coalition` (union observer `_∪_`)

| L-7
| Self-defeating privacy compositions cannot be built as programs. A typed command writes a value depending on both fields, through its expression or through the guards around it, only into a variable that the client alone may read. Lintel's two fields include no such variable, so no typed command writes the link at all.
| PROVEN
| `src/Lintel/Composition.agda`: `writes-flow`, `link-written-to-client` (any labelling), `no-link-written` (Lintel's fields); `tests/reject/ProgramLink.agda` (explicit), `tests/reject/ProgramLinkImplicit.agda` (implicit), with positive controls `tests/accept/ProgramField.agda` and `ProgramFieldGuarded.agda`

| A-1
| Non-collusion: no party holds both the proxy and the target role. TINI holds for coalitions too (L-6), but the confinement of the link (L-4, L-7) is per role: proxy and target together see the address and the query name, each by a permitted flow, and can join them outside any program.
| ASSUMED
| Non-collusion, as in proven-servers `protocols/proven-odns` (`ODNS.Types.Role`); the gap is proved: `Lintel.Instance.collusion-gap`
|===

The `Lattice` record is defined once, in secret-types. Lintel proves the laws and
supplies the instance. `just proof-check-lintel` checks everything above (it
runs `scripts/fetch-siblings.sh` then `scripts/check.sh`), and so does the
`Agda proofs` workflow: Agda 2.6.4.3, `--safe --without-K --no-libraries
--double-check`, secret-types pinned at `ccdcb3e`.
